Super Mona Kart: third-party characters
========================================

The racers below are original pixel art drawn for this game (Team2/make_assets.py),
based on open-source mascots. They are adaptations ("modified"), not copies of
the original image files. The mascots appear as tributes; no endorsement by any
project or company is implied, and none of the projects' logos are used.

Mona, Cluck, Sciuri, Buzz
  Characters of the GitHub Universe badge apps (Flappy, Plucky Cluck, Snarky
  Sciuridae, Bee Amazed). Side and front views reuse those apps' sprites from
  this repository.

Tux (Linux)
  Based on Tux by Larry Ewing (lewing@isc.tamu.edu), drawn with The GIMP.
  Terms: "Permission to use and/or modify this image is granted provided you
  acknowledge me lewing@isc.tamu.edu and The GIMP if someone asks."
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tux_(mascot)

Ferris (Rust)
  Based on Ferris the Rustacean by Karen Rustad Tolva, dedicated to the public
  domain under CC0 1.0. No attribution is required; it is given as a courtesy.
  Source: https://rustacean.net/

Gopher (Go)

Duke (Java)
